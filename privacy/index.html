BLOC Planner Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

BLOC Planner is made by Cory Nekvinda. This policy covers the BLOC Planner app for Mac, iPad and Windows.

Data Not Collected

BLOC Planner does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Works Offline

The app doesn't need an internet connection and doesn't send your planners anywhere. Your planners, saved templates and projects stay on your device, in the app's own storage. Files you export or save (PDF or project files) go only to the location you choose.

Purchases

The app is a one-time purchase handled by the App Store (or Microsoft Store). The store processes your payment under its own privacy policy, and I don't receive your payment details.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: Support@corystemplates.com
