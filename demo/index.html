How to use Cory's Templates

You can try the builder here. In the full app, you save pages as a PDF to use on a tablet (GoodNotes, Notability, reMarkable, Kindle Scribe…) or to print.

  1. Get a planner going. Click Build a Planner for a full year, month or week with every page made and linked. Or click New → Start from scratch for an empty page.
  2. Pick your page size. With nothing selected, the right-hand panel shows Page Properties — choose iPad, Letter, A4 or your e-ink tablet under Page size.
  3. Add boxes. Click anything in the left list (Notes, To-Do List, Habit Tracker…) and it lands on the page. Drag it to move it; drag the small blue squares to resize it.
  4. Change a box. Click it, then use the right-hand panel: title, colors, number of lines, and where it links to.
  5. Add pages. Use + Page. The Pages tab on the left lists them all; use ↑ ↓ to reorder.
  6. Check your links. Turn on Test Links and click buttons and dates — it jumps just like the finished PDF will.
  7. Export and print. Those are turned off in this demo. They’re available in the full app on the Microsoft Store.

Your work saves itself in this browser as you go. Use Save to write it now, and Load (or My Projects) to open it again. It stays on this website only — clearing your browser data, or opening the demo on a different website, removes it. Downloading a project file is turned off in the demo.

GoodNotes, Notability, Noteshelf (iPad)

  1. Choose iPad / Tablet as the page size, then click Export → Save or share PDF.
  2. Send the PDF to your iPad — AirDrop, email it to yourself, or put it in iCloud Drive / Google Drive / Dropbox.
  3. On the iPad, open the file and choose Open in GoodNotes (or Notability), or use Import inside the app.
  4. Tap the buttons and dates to jump around. If a tap draws instead of jumping, switch GoodNotes to read-only mode (or turn on “Apple Pencil only” drawing and tap with your finger).

reMarkable, Supernote, Kindle Scribe, BOOX

Choose your device under Page size (and the “E-ink gray” tab colors on the Pages tab), export, then send the PDF with the device's own app or website.

Printing

Choose US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) or A4 under Page size before exporting. Print the PDF at Actual size (100%).

  • Ctrl/⌘ Z undo · Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z or Ctrl Y redo
  • Ctrl/⌘ D copy the selected box
  • Delete remove the selected box
  • Arrow keys nudge the selected box (hold Shift to move faster)
  • Page Up / Page Down previous / next page

Snap lines boxes up neatly with each other and the page edges. Turn it off for free placement.

Changing the page size later scales every page to fit — but it is easiest to pick it first.
