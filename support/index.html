Support

Questions, problems, or ideas for BLOC Planner or any of my planners? Email me:

Support@corystemplates.com

This is a one-person shop, so you're writing to the person who built it. I read everything and reply as fast as I can — usually within a couple of days.

To help me help you

Tell me which app or planner you're using (Mac, iPad, or Windows), what you were doing, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by the store you bought from — the App Store or the Microsoft Store — so refund requests go through them. If something's wrong with the app itself, email me first and I'll make it right if I can.

Your files

BLOC Planner works offline and keeps everything on your device. Your planners are saved in the app's own storage, and exports go only where you choose to save them.
